Common questions

Do I need a patent attorney instead?

Andrew is a registered patent agent. Patent agents can prepare and file patent applications with the USPTO and advise on patent strategy. For contracts, NDAs, licensing terms, disputes or other legal advice, you need an attorney, and Andrew will say so when a question crosses that line.

Is it too early to think about patents?

If you are about to demo, pitch, post or ship, it is usually the right time. The cheapest useful step often has to happen before that first disclosure, not after it.

What if I have already shown investors or posted a demo?

Get in touch soon. The US gives inventors a one-year grace period after their own public disclosure, but many other countries do not, so the options narrow the longer you wait.

What does it cost?

It depends on what you need. See what a provisional patent costs a startup for current USPTO fees and typical ranges, or email Andrew with your stage and ask for a quote.

Do I have to file a patent to work with Andrew?

No. Sometimes the useful answer is what not to file yet, or what to keep private instead.
